In this project I stepped out of my comfort zone: not only shooting partially nude portraits, but also working in Photoshop to alter the image, breaking my rule of never digitally altering a photograph.

The idea of double exposure resonated with me even more once I started shooting film. Maybe it’s a cliché, but I wanted to melt Montréal’s landmarks into portraits of the people who live here.

I’m looking to expand this series. If you’d like to be part of it, [get in touch](https://jpvalery.me/contact/photography) to set up a shoot.
