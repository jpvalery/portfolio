New York, New York. A city that can’t really be described, only experienced. How do you convey the dynamics of people and concrete, the play of shadow and light? Navigating from Brooklyn to Manhattan and on to Coney Island like a modern adventurer, I made this series about the grandeur of the urban jungle.
