## About me

I’m a self-taught photographer based in Montréal, Québec. My passion for photography was born from a deep desire to share and document the beauty I see around me.

My work has been featured in international publications many times. I published my first book, *An American Road Trip*, in 2016, followed in 2018 by *Mystic Mists of Massachusetts*.

In 2017 and 2018, I was named a Community All-Star on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/@jpvalery), where my photos have been viewed more than 300 million times and downloaded more than 1.8 million times.

In 2019, I founded the [Montréal Photo Club](https://montrealphoto.club) to organize photography events and gatherings in Montréal.

In 2020, I released [lookbutnostopping.com](https://lookbutnostopping.com), an interactive story built from photos of my trip across Newfoundland the year before.

For commissions, collaborations or licensing, or just to say hello, [send me a message](https://jpvalery.me/contact/photography).

## My artist statement

I believe in a very natural photography, close to the standards of photojournalism. My photographs are not digitally altered: not retouched, photoshopped or transformed.

I try to capture images that transmit emotion and tell stories. I’d define my vision as almost naturalist but not emotionless in the depiction of my reality.

> There’s a lot of beauty in ordinary things. Isn’t that kind of the point?
